# Problem 18: Maximum Path Sum I

**Difficulty**: 5%
**Category**: Dynamic Programming / Graph Theory (DAG)

## Problem Statement

By starting at the top of the triangle below and moving to adjacent numbers
on the row below, find the maximum total from top to bottom.

```
75
95 64
17 47 82
18 35 87 10
20 04 82 47 65
19 01 23 75 03 34
88 02 77 73 07 63 67
99 65 04 28 06 16 70 92
41 41 26 56 83 40 80 70 33
41 48 72 33 47 32 37 16 94 29
53 71 44 65 25 43 91 52 97 51 14
70 11 33 28 77 73 17 78 39 68 17 57
91 71 52 38 17 14 91 43 58 50 27 29 48
63 66 04 68 89 53 67 30 73 16 69 87 40 31
04 62 98 27 23 09 70 98 73 93 38 53 60 04 23
```

**NOTE**: As there are only 16,384 routes, it is possible to solve this problem
by trying every route. However, Problem 67, which contains a triangle with
one-hundred rows, cannot be solved by brute force, and requires a clever method! Do you have such a method? ;o)

## Examples

Small triangle (answer = 23):
```
   3
  7 4
 2 4 6
8 5 9 3
```
Path: 3 → 7 → 4 → 9 = **23**

## Why Greedy Fails

```
    5
   9  1
  1  1  100
```
Greedy (always pick largest): 5 → 9 → 1 = **15**
DP (consider full subtree): 5 → 1 → 100 = **106**

## Constraints

- 15 rows, up to 16,384 possible paths
- At each step, move to either adjacent number in the row below
