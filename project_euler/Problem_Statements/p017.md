# Problem 17: Number Letter Counts

**Difficulty**: 5%
**Category**: Combinatorics / String Enumeration

## Problem Statement

If the numbers 1 to 5 are written out in words: one, two, three, four, five,
then there are 3 + 3 + 5 + 4 + 4 = 19 letters used in total.

If all the numbers from 1 to 1,000 (one thousand) inclusive were written out
in words, how many letters would be used?

**NOTE**: Do not count spaces or hyphens. For example, 342 (three hundred and
forty-two) contains 23 letters and 115 (one hundred and fifteen) contains 20
letters. The use of "and" when writing out numbers is in accordance with
British usage.

## Examples

| Number | Words | Letters |
|--------|-------|---------|
| 1–5 | one, two, three, four, five | 3+3+5+4+4 = 19 |
| 115 | one hundred and fifteen | 3+7+3+7 = 20 |
| 342 | three hundred and forty-two | 5+7+3+5+3 = 23 |
| 1000 | one thousand | 3+8 = 11 |

## Constraints

- Count letters only (no spaces, no hyphens)
- British convention: "and" between hundreds and remainder
- 1 ≤ n ≤ 1,000
