# Problem 28: Number Spiral Diagonals

**Difficulty**: 5%
**Category**: Number Theory / Closed-Form Summation

## Problem Statement

Starting with the number 1 and moving to the right in a clockwise direction,
a 5 by 5 spiral is formed as follows:

```text
21 22 23 24 25
20  7  8  9 10
19  6  1  2 11
18  5  4  3 12
17 16 15 14 13
```

It can be verified that the sum of the numbers on the diagonals is 101.

What is the sum of the numbers on the diagonals of a 1001 by 1001 spiral?

## Examples

- 1×1 spiral: diagonal sum = 1
- 3×3 spiral: 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 25
- 5×5 spiral: 25 + 13 + 17 + 21 + 25 = 101

## Mathematical Foundation

For an n×n spiral (n odd), layer k has four corner values:
- Top-right: (2k+1)²
- Top-left: (2k+1)² − 2k
- Bottom-left: (2k+1)² − 4k
- Bottom-right: (2k+1)² − 6k

Layer corner sum = 4(2k+1)² − 12k = 16k² + 4k + 4

Using standard summation formulas (Σk² and Σk), the total has a **closed-form**:

Total = 1 + 8m(m+1)(2m+1)/3 + 2m(m+1) + 4m, where m = (n−1)/2

## Constraints

- n = 1001 (odd)
- Answer: **669,171,001**
