# Problem 4: Largest Palindrome Product

**Difficulty**: Easy  
**Published**: November 16, 2001

## Problem Statement

A palindromic number reads the same both ways. The largest palindrome made from the product of two 2-digit numbers is 9009 = 91 × 99.

**Find the largest palindrome made from the product of two 3-digit numbers.**

## Examples

**2-digit case**:
- Largest palindrome: 9009
- Factorization: 91 × 99
- 9009 reads the same forwards and backwards

## Constraints

- Both factors must be 3-digit numbers (100 ≤ n ≤ 999)
- Result must be palindromic (reads same forwards/backwards)

## Approach Hints

1. **Brute force**: Test all products of 3-digit numbers
2. **Palindrome check**: Convert to string or use mathematical reversal
3. **Optimization**: Start from 999 and work down (early termination)
4. **Search space**: ~405,000 products to check (900 × 900 / 2)

## Mathematical Insights

- Largest possible product: 999 × 999 = 998,001
- Smallest possible product: 100 × 100 = 10,000
- Search space can be reduced by starting from max and working down
- Can skip cases where `a < b` (symmetry)
