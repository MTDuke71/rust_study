# Problem 3: Largest Prime Factor

**Difficulty**: 5%  
**Category**: Prime Numbers, Number Theory, Factorization

## Problem Statement

The prime factors of 13195 are 5, 7, 13 and 29.

**What is the largest prime factor of the number 600851475143?**

## Examples

- Number: 13195
- Prime factorization: 5 × 7 × 13 × 29
- Largest prime factor: 29

## Constraints

- Target number: 600,851,475,143
- Find the largest prime factor (not just any factor)
- Result must be prime

## Approach Hints

- **Brute force**: Check all numbers up to √n for primality and divisibility
- **Trial division**: Divide out small factors first
  1. Remove all factors of 2
  2. Try odd numbers 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, ... up to √n
  3. For each divisor found, divide it out completely
  4. If remainder > 1 after all divisions, it's the largest prime factor
- **Optimization**: No need for primality testing if you divide out factors completely

## Mathematical Foundation

- Any composite number n has a prime factor ≤ √n
- After dividing out all factors up to √n, any remainder > 1 must be prime
- Time complexity: O(√n) worst case, much faster in practice
