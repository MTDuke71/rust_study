title: Rust Book Integration
---

Rust Book Integration tracks how concepts from the official Rust Book are reinforced across missions, daily study notes, Advent of Code work, and tutorials. The goal is to ensure every major chapter shows up in at least one mission, one daily-study example, and one zettelkasten concept note.

This note serves as a coordination hub rather than a deep-dive chapter summary. When a chapter drives new code or study material, add a short bullet here and link back to the specific mission, daily note, or AoC problem.
