# Sieve of Eratosthenes

**Algorithm**: Ancient algorithm for finding all prime numbers up to a specified integer.

---

## Overview

The **Sieve of Eratosthenes** is an efficient algorithm for finding all primes up to a limit `n`. Time complexity: O(n log log n).
