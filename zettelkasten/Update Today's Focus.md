# 📝 Update Today's Focus

*Simple guide to manually update the Today's Focus note*

---

## 🔄 **How to Update Today's Focus**

### **Step 1: Check Current Date**
Look at your computer's date or use this: **Today is Tuesday, October 14, 2025**

### **Step 2: Update the Note**
1. Open `zettelkasten/Today's Focus.md`
2. Update the date in the "Current Date" section
3. Copy the day's content from `MONTHLY_CALENDAR.md`
4. Update the links to point to today's files
