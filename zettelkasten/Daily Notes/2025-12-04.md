## 🔧 **Technical Insights**
- **Iterative erosion pattern**: Collect all valid cells → remove all → repeat until empty
- **New `Vec` per iteration**: Rust idiom - `let accessible: Vec<_> = ...collect()` in loop creates fresh allocation each iteration (old one dropped automatically)
- **Cellular automaton ≠ flood fill**: Each cell evaluated independently by immediate neighbors, not graph traversal
- **Object safety rules**: No `Self` return, no generic methods, all methods dispatchable
