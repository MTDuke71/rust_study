## Technical Insights

### AoC 2016 Day 1 — Two Modes of Walking

Part 1 and Part 2 solve the same navigation, but at different granularities:

```
Part 1: jump by steps (O(n) instructions, O(1) space)
Part 2: step one block at a time (O(n×s) blocks, O(positions) space)
```

The `HashSet` revisit check is what makes Part 2 ~8× slower, not algorithmic complexity.

### x86 ABI — Volatile vs Callee-Saved Registers

Key rule for Windows x86 stdcall:
- **Volatile** (caller must save if needed): EAX, ECX, EDX
- **Callee-saved** (function will restore): EBX, ESI, EDI, EBP

After any `CALL`/`INVOKE`, assume EAX/ECX/EDX are clobbered. This was the bug that caused the number to not print — ECX held the digit count but was destroyed by the preceding `WriteFile` call.
