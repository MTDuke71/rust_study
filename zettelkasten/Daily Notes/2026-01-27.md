## 🔧 **Technical Insights**

### Algorithm Comparison: Iterative LCM vs Prime Factorization

**Iterative LCM** (chosen):
```rust
(1..=n).fold(1, lcm)  // Elegant, functional, idiomatic!
```
- ✅ Simple, $O(1)$ space
- ✅ Easy to understand
- ❌ $O(n \log M)$ time

**Prime Factorization** (alternative):
- ✅ More efficient: $O(n \log \log n)$
- ❌ More complex (~30 lines)
- ❌ Requires sieve, $O(n)$ space

**Decision**: Simplicity wins for n=20. Lesson: Choose simplest approach that works.

### Search Optimization Patterns (PE4)

**Techniques**:
1. Reverse iteration (start from max)
2. Early termination (break when can't improve)
3. Symmetry (avoid duplicate work)
4. Bounds checking

**Result**: 98.8% reduction in work (810K → 10K products checked)

### Euclidean Algorithm Understanding

**Why it works**: Any divisor of $(a, b)$ also divides $(b, a \bmod b)$

**Complexity**: $O(\log \min(a, b))$ - logarithmic!

**Applications**: LCM, fractions, modular inverses, cryptography
