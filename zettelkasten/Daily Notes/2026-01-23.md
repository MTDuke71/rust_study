## 🔧 **Technical Insights**

### Graph Contraction Technique
- **Problem**: Longest path is NP-hard (exponential complexity)
- **Naive approach**: DFS on 20K tiles caused stack overflow (7,000 depth)
- **Solution**: Identify junctions (degree ≠ 2), collapse corridors into weighted edges
- **Result**: 35 junctions vs 20,000 tiles, recursion depth 35 vs 7,000
- **Learning**: State space reduction via preprocessing can transform impossible → solvable

### Backtracking Pattern (HashSet)
- Use `HashSet<Coord>` for visited tracking (O(1) operations)
- Critical: `visited.remove(&coord)` after recursion (backtrack!)
- Pattern: Make choice → Recurse → Undo choice
- Explores all possible paths while preventing cycles
