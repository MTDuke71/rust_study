## 🔧 **Technical Insights**

**Parse-Once Pattern**: Measured 49% speedup (25.6µs combined vs 49.8µs separate)
- Critical for days with complex parsing
- Separates concerns: parsing vs solving logic
- Enables easy testing of individual parts

**pdqsort Deep Dive**:
- Hybrid: quicksort + heapsort fallback + insertion sort (≤24 elements)
- Pattern detection prevents O(n²) on sorted/reverse/duplicate patterns
- Block partitioning improves cache utilization
- Guarantees O(n log n) worst-case (unlike classic quicksort)

**Rust Iterator Mastery**:
- `.split("\n\n")` for blank-line group parsing
- `.filter_map(|x| x.parse().ok())` for robust error handling
- `sort_unstable_by(|a, b| b.cmp(a))` for descending order
