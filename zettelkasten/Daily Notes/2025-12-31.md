## 🔧 **Technical Insights**

### **Higher-Ranked Trait Bounds (HRTB) Philosophy**
- # **Generic Memoization Cache Patterns**
- **Cache hits require repeated computation**: Fibonacci (overlapping subproblems), factorial (cache reuse), multiple queries (shared substrings)
- **String decomposition creates unique substrings**: Single-query pattern matching has 0% hit rate because each substring is different as it gets shorter
- **FnOnce trait bound**: Most flexible closure trait - allows consuming captured values, used when closure called once per cache miss
- **Connection to existing knowledge**: Referenced [[Closures in Rust]] zettelkasten note for Fn/FnMut/FnOnce trait family
