### Bidirectional Search Optimization

Key insight: **Exponential reduction** in nodes explored when searching from both ends
- Single-directional: $10^6$ nodes at depth 6, branching factor 10
- Bidirectional: $2 \times 10^3 = 2,000$ nodes (500x faster!)
- Critical for large graphs with known start and goal nodes
