## 🔧 **Technical Insights**

### Future Trait Mechanics
```rust
pub trait Future {
    type Output;
    fn poll(self: Pin<&mut Self>, cx: &mut Context<'_>) -> Poll<Self::Output>;
}
```
- `Pin<&mut Self>` prevents self-referential futures from moving
- Most types are `Unpin` (safe to move even when pinned)
- Async blocks are `!Unpin` (must stay pinned)
