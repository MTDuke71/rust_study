### MOD vs % Deep Understanding ✅
Clarified difference between Python's `%` (true modulo) and Rust's `%` (remainder):
- **Python**: `(-3) % 5 = 2` (always non-negative, floored division)
- **Rust/C**: `(-3) % 5 = -3` (keeps sign, truncated division)
- **Day 1 Solution**: Uses signed arithmetic correctly - `dial / 100` gives correct rotation count
