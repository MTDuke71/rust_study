---
title: Two-Pointer Techniques
---

Two-pointer techniques solve problems by moving one or two indices through an array or string, often from opposite ends or at different speeds. They are especially powerful for linear-time solutions to searching, partitioning, and comparison problems on sorted or structured data.

This note ties common interview patterns (pair sums, palindrome checks, merging, in-place partitioning) back to concrete Rust implementations using slices, indices, and iterator pairs.
